The problem

Cabin crew pay is complicated. Payday shouldn’t be.

Basic, sectors, commission, extras and deductions all move every month and none of it is obvious until the payslip lands. Working it out by hand means a spreadsheet, an old roster and a lost afternoon.

Was that airport standby paid? Why is this month lower than last? Did my sales commission land? How many sectors did I actually fly? Is my nightstop allowance in there? Was that swap actually worth it? Am I better off picking up that day? JetWages answers all of these.

How it works

Upload. Review. Track.

Set up once with your payslips, then keep your roster and pay in sync as you fly.

1 Upload

Drop in your payslip PDF

Save your payslip (and supplementary payslip), then tap Select PDF File. JetWages extracts your salary information automatically.

2 Review

Confirm the figures

Check the extracted basic pay, sector rates and deductions, then move on to your supplementary payslip and leave estimate. You’re in control of every value before it goes into your history.

3 Track

See it on your calendar

Upload or sync your roster so each day shows flights, standby, leave or days off. Open a day to edit duties, log sales or add extras, then jump into Pie View for the month’s breakdown.

Why I built it

I tracked my pay on a spreadsheet for years.

It worked. It also ate an evening every month. Here’s the difference.

  • ×Typed by hand. Every sector, every allowance, every month.

  • ×No history worth having. Comparing months means scrolling and squinting.
  • ×Errors are invisible. A wrong formula quietly follows you all year.

JetWages

  • ✓Reads the payslip for you. Upload the PDF and review the figures.
  • ✓Follows your roster. Edit a day, log sales, add extras — pay stays in sync.
  • ✓Months side by side. Stats shows trends and odd months at a glance.
  • ✓Shows the monthly earning. Pie View breaks out Basic, Sector, Commission, Extras and Deductions.
  • ✓Stays on your device. No account, no cloud copy of your pay history.

About

Built by crew, for crew.

Hi, I’m a flight attendant. Like most crew, I never really knew what I’d be paid until the payslip landed.

Around late 2016 I started tracking it all myself on a spreadsheet: flights, sales, the lot. It worked, but it took ages.

Eventually I thought there had to be a better way, so I built one. That’s how JetWages started. It took a lot of planning, a lot of redesigning, and a lot of feedback from crew along the way, but it’s been worth it.

Privacy

Your payslip is yours.

You’re uploading a document with your name and your pay on it. Here’s exactly what happens.

On your device

Roster, pay history, sales and settings stay on your phone. There’s no JetWages account or cloud copy of your pay data.

PDFs aren’t kept

When a file needs parsing, it’s read over a secure connection and deleted as soon as parsing finishes.

Not shared

No advertising, no tracking pixels, no selling your data to third parties.

FAQ

Questions crew usually ask.

Does it cost anything?

JetWages is free to use. If it’s helped you, a coffee keeps the project going, there’s a link on this page and in the app.

Which airlines are supported?

easyJet UK for now. Support for other airlines depends on getting hold of their payslip and roster formats, so requests genuinely help.

What do you do with my payslip?

It’s read so the app can work out your pay figures. On iOS (and sometimes Android) the PDF is sent to a secure parsing server, then deleted. Your roster and pay history stay on your device. Full detail is in the privacy policy.

Where is my data stored?

On your device. JetWages has no login and no cloud database for your pay or roster. You can export a JSON backup from Settings if you want a copy.

Do I have to upload every month?

A new supplementary payslip easch month to ensure saes commission is paid correctly.

Will it work if I’ve swapped or changed duties?

Yes. Open the day and use Edit to fix duties, Sales for onboard sales (including crew-count changes from an AC swap), and Extras for things like upranks.

Who’s behind it?

Just me. I’m a flight attendant and I build JetWages outside flying hours, which is also why replies sometimes take a day or two.

How do I get help?

Send a message through the feedback form, or email support@jetwages.com. It comes straight to me.
