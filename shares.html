Month-end contribution

Contributions are taken at the end of each month. Court Sanction is usually 1–2 working days before the Effective Date, and Choice B is exercised about 20 days after it. If that 20-day window runs past the end of the month, Choice B picks up that month’s contribution.

How to calculate it yourself
  1. Count your payments. Count the monthly payments made before the Effective Date month. Payments start at the end of August of the scheme year and end at the end of July three years later.
  2. Work out the amount saved. Amount saved each month × number of payments.
  3. Work out your shares. Amount saved ÷ your option price (not the £7.15 offer price). Round down to a whole number.
  4. Work out what you are paid for your shares. Shares × £7.15.
  5. Work out the savings returned. Amount saved − (shares × option price).
  6. Work out the compensation. Add the payments left (up to 12) to your amount saved, and divide by your option price to get the shares you could have bought. Take away the shares from step 3, then multiply the extra shares by (£7.15 − option price).
Example: SAYE24, £100 a month, Effective Date in March 2027 1. Payments from August 2024 to February 2027: 31 2. Amount saved: £100 × 31 = £3,100 3. Shares: £3,100 ÷ £3.61 = 858 (round down) 4. Paid for your shares: 858 × £7.15 = £6,134.70 5. Savings left over and returned: £3,100 − (858 × £3.61) = £2.62 6. Compensation: only 5 payments are left, so £100 × 5 = £500 more. Shares with the full £3,600: £3,600 ÷ £3.61 = 997. Extra shares: 997 − 858 = 139. Compensation: 139 × (£7.15 − £3.61) = £492.06 Total paid to you: £6,134.70 + £2.62 + £492.06 = £6,629.38

Any amount saved that is not used to buy shares comes back to you. Choice A must be elected on the ESP Portal by 5pm on 15 November 2026. If you do nothing, the options lapse.
