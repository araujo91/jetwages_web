Privacy & Data Policy – JetWages

Last updated: 14 July 2026

1. Overview

JetWages is an independent project operated in the United Kingdom. This policy explains how JetWages handles your data in compliance with UK and EU data protection laws (UK GDPR / EU GDPR).

JetWages is designed so that your roster, pay history, sales, and settings stay on your device. There is no JetWages login and no JetWages backend that stores your pay data. The only times data leaves your device are described in the sections below (for example, optional PDF parsing, subscriptions, or forms you choose to submit).

2. Data Controller

Email: data@jetwages.com
Location: United Kingdom

3. Local-First Model

JetWages follows a local-first and ephemeral-processing model:

  • Roster, pay breakdowns, sales, commission, extras, and settings are stored only on your device (using the platform’s secure local storage).
  • There is no JetWages account and no cloud database for your pay or roster data.
  • When PDF parsing needs server assistance (see §5), only the PDF file is sent — not your name, email, or other personal details.
  • Files sent for parsing are processed over HTTPS and deleted immediately after successful parsing. No files, parsed content, or logs are retained on the parsing server after the process completes.

4. On-Device Data

The app stores data locally to power features such as your calendar view, pay history, sales reminders, theme preferences, and calendar-sync settings. This includes, for example:

  • Monthly roster and per-day flight, duty, sales, commission, and extras data
  • Pay history (salary, benefits, pension, effective dates)
  • Country, currency, and active airline settings
  • Sales reminder schedules and notification preferences
  • Calendar sync preferences (which calendar you connected)
  • Local setup metrics (see §12) — stored on device only

Uninstalling the app removes this data from your device. You can also clear roster data from the Debug screen in the app.

5. PDF Processing

When you upload a payslip, supplementary payslip, or schedule PDF, JetWages extracts text so pay data can be parsed. Behaviour depends on your platform:

  • iOS: PDFs are sent to a secure HTTPS parsing server operated for JetWages. The request contains only the PDF file and an authentication token — no user ID, account data, or device identifier. The server returns extracted text; the file is deleted from the server as soon as parsing finishes (usually within seconds).
  • Android: The app first tries to extract text locally on your device using PDF.js (loaded from a CDN). If local extraction fails or the PDF has no text layer (for example, a scanned payslip), the app falls back to the same secure HTTPS parsing server used on iOS, under the same conditions.

Extracted text is used only within the app on your device. JetWages does not send parsed pay data to any server.

6. Device Permissions

JetWages may request the following permissions. You can deny any optional permission; related features will be limited but the rest of the app remains usable.

  • Calendar (read-only): If you enable calendar sync, the app reads events from calendars you explicitly select (for example, your eCrew roster). It reads event titles, dates, and notes (such as debrief times) to build your roster on device. The app does not create or edit calendar events, and no calendar content is sent to JetWages.
  • Location: Used only to help determine your timezone when scheduling sales reminders at the correct local time. Coordinates are not stored or sent to JetWages.
  • Notifications: Sales reminders are scheduled as local notifications on your device. There is no JetWages push server; the operating system delivers reminders at the scheduled time. Reminder data (date, debrief time, route) stays on your device.

7. Backup and Export

You can export a JSON backup of your app data from Settings. The file is written to your device and shared via the system share sheet (for example, email or cloud storage). JetWages does not receive or store backup files.

Backup files contain all of your app data (roster, pay, reminders, and settings). Treat them as sensitive and store them securely. Importing a backup is processed entirely on your device.

8. Subscriptions (Jetwages Pro)

JetWages offers an optional Jetwages Pro subscription (monthly or yearly) managed through RevenueCat and processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play Store.

  • RevenueCat receives subscription-related data such as purchase status, store receipts, and an anonymous app user identifier used by the SDK.
  • JetWages does not send roster, pay, salary, or other in-app data to RevenueCat.
  • There is no JetWages login; subscription management is handled by the app store and RevenueCat.
  • RevenueCat’s privacy policy applies to their processing: revenuecat.com/privacy.

9. Third-Party Services

In addition to the parsing server and RevenueCat (above), JetWages may interact with the following third parties:

  • Apple App Store / Google Play: Payment processing for subscriptions.
  • Cloudflare CDN: On Android, the PDF.js library is loaded from cdnjs.cloudflare.com for local PDF text extraction. No payslip content is sent to the CDN.
  • Google Forms / Google Sheets: Some optional forms (for example, feedback or wage-component research) are hosted using Google tools. Responses are stored in Google Sheets under our Google Workspace account and are not linked to your in-app data.

JetWages does not use third-party advertising, tracking pixels, or behavioural analytics SDKs.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

  • No uploaded PDFs or parsed content are stored on JetWages parsing servers after processing completes.
  • All roster and pay data remain on your device until you delete it, clear data in the app, or uninstall.
  • Form responses (including any wage component details and, where provided, your email address) stored in Google Sheets are kept only for as long as needed for testing, support, or improving JetWages, or until you ask us to delete them.
  • Subscription records are retained by RevenueCat and the app store according to their own policies.

11. Email Communications

If you voluntarily provide your email (for example, for beta access, feedback, updates, or as part of a wage component/setup form), it is stored securely and used only for communication about JetWages.

Some forms may ask whether you agree to be contacted. In those cases, your email is only collected and stored if you select “Yes” or otherwise clearly consent to being contacted.

You can unsubscribe at any time, and your email (and any associated form responses) will be deleted upon request or at the end of the relevant testing or feedback phase.

12. Local Setup Metrics

The app records a small amount of local-only setup information on your device (for example, whether you used a backup or manual setup, file counts, and setup duration). This is stored in device storage and is not sent automatically to JetWages or any third party. Export is user-initiated only (for example, via the debug/analytics export feature).

13. Legal Basis for Processing

  • Consent: Uploading PDFs for parsing, submitting forms, providing your email, and granting optional device permissions (calendar, location, notifications).
  • Contract: Processing necessary to provide Jetwages Pro subscriptions you have purchased.
  • Legitimate interests: Operating and securing the parsing server and app, and responding to support requests — balanced against your rights.

You may withdraw consent at any time by contacting data@jetwages.com or by disabling permissions in your device settings. Withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

14. Security Measures

All transfers use HTTPS encryption. Uploaded PDFs are deleted immediately after parsing. Local app data is stored using secure device-level storage. Form responses in Google Sheets are protected by Google’s security measures and restricted to authorised access only.

15. International Data Transfers

Some third-party processors (for example, RevenueCat in the United States, Google for forms, and CDN providers) may process limited data outside the UK/EEA. Where required, appropriate safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses) apply under the processor’s terms and UK GDPR requirements.

16. Your Rights

  • Access the limited data we hold (for example, your email and any form responses, if provided)
  • Request correction or deletion of your contact data and form responses
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Object to or restrict certain processing where applicable
  • Data portability for data you have provided to us directly
  • Lodge a complaint with your data protection authority
