Privacy & Data Policy – JetWages

Last updated: 20 July 2026

1. Overview

JetWages is an independent app run from the United Kingdom. This page explains what happens to your data. We follow UK and EU privacy laws (UK GDPR / EU GDPR).

Your roster, pay history, sales, and settings stay on your phone or tablet. JetWages has no login and no cloud storage for your pay data. Data only leaves your device in the cases described below — for example, optional PDF parsing or forms you choose to send.

2. Who is responsible

Email: data@jetwages.com
Location: United Kingdom

3. Your data stays on your device

JetWages is built so your data stays with you:

  • Your roster, pay breakdowns, sales, commission, extras, and settings are stored only on your device.
  • There is no JetWages account and no cloud database for your pay or roster.
  • If PDF parsing needs our server (see section 5), we only send the PDF file — not your name, email, or other personal details.
  • Files sent for parsing travel over a secure connection (HTTPS) and are deleted as soon as parsing finishes. We do not keep the file, the parsed text, or logs afterwards.

4. What the app stores on your device

The app keeps data on your device so features work — for example your calendar view, pay history, sales reminders, theme, and calendar sync. This can include:

  • Your monthly roster and day-by-day flight, duty, sales, commission, and extras
  • Pay history (salary, benefits, pension, and when they started)
  • Country, currency, and airline settings
  • Sales reminder times and notification choices
  • Which calendar you connected for sync
  • Simple setup notes (see section 11) — on your device only

Uninstalling the app removes this data from your device.

5. PDF processing

When you upload a payslip, supplementary payslip, or schedule PDF, JetWages reads the text so it can work out your pay. What happens depends on your phone:

  • iOS: The PDF is sent to a secure JetWages parsing server over HTTPS. The request includes only the PDF and a security token — no user ID, account data, or device ID. The server sends back the extracted text, then deletes the file (usually within seconds).
  • Android: The app first tries to read the PDF on your device using PDF.js (loaded from the internet). If that fails, for example with a scanned payslip, it uses the same secure parsing server as on iOS, with the same rules.

The text that comes back is used only in the app on your device. JetWages does not send your parsed pay figures to any other server.

6. Device permissions

JetWages may ask for the permissions below. You can say no to any optional one. Some features will not work, but the rest of the app still will.

  • Calendar (read-only): If you turn on calendar sync, the app reads events from calendars you choose (for example your eCrew roster). It uses titles, dates, and notes (such as debrief times) to build your roster on your device. It does not create or change calendar events, and nothing from your calendar is sent to JetWages.
  • Location: Used only to work out your timezone so sales reminders fire at the right local time. Your location is not saved or sent to JetWages.
  • Notifications: Sales reminders are local notifications on your device. There is no JetWages push server; your phone shows the reminder at the right time. Reminder details (date, debrief time, route) stay on your device.

7. Backup and export

You can export a JSON backup of your app data from Settings. The file is saved on your device and shared with the system share sheet (for example email or cloud storage). JetWages does not receive or store backup files.

A backup includes all your app data (roster, pay, reminders, and settings). Keep it safe. Importing a backup happens entirely on your device.

8. Other services we use

As well as the parsing server (above), JetWages may use:

  • Cloudflare CDN: On Android, the PDF.js library is loaded from cdnjs.cloudflare.com so PDFs can be read on your device. No payslip content is sent to the CDN.
  • Google Forms / Google Sheets: Optional forms (for example feedback or wage research) may use Google tools. Answers are stored in Google Sheets under our Google account and are not linked to your data in the app.

JetWages does not use advertising, tracking pixels, or behaviour-tracking tools.

9. How long we keep data

  • Uploaded PDFs and parsed content are not kept on JetWages servers after processing finishes.
  • Roster and pay data stay on your device until you delete them, clear data in the app, or uninstall.
  • Form answers (including any wage details and, if you gave it, your email) in Google Sheets are kept only as long as we need them for testing, support, or improving JetWages — or until you ask us to delete them.

10. Email

If you choose to give us your email (for example for beta access, feedback, updates, or a wage setup form), we store it securely and use it only to contact you about JetWages.

Some forms ask if you want to be contacted. We only keep your email for that purpose if you say yes or clearly agree.

You can ask us to stop emailing you at any time. We will delete your email (and related form answers) when you ask, or when that testing or feedback stage ends.

11. Setup notes on your device

The app keeps a small amount of local-only setup information on your device (for example whether you used a backup or set up by hand, how many files you used, and how long setup took). This stays in your device storage and is not sent automatically to JetWages or anyone else. You only share it if you choose to export it (for example from the debug/analytics export).

12. Why we process data (legal basis)

  • Consent: You choose to upload PDFs, submit forms, share your email, or allow optional permissions (calendar, location, notifications).
  • Legitimate interests: Running and securing the parsing server and app, and answering support requests — always balanced against your rights.

You can withdraw consent at any time by emailing data@jetwages.com or by turning off permissions in your device settings. That does not undo anything that was already done lawfully before you withdrew.

13. Security

All data we transfer uses HTTPS encryption. Uploaded PDFs are deleted right after parsing. App data on your device uses the phone’s secure storage. Form answers in Google Sheets are protected by Google’s security and only people we authorise can access them.

14. Data outside the UK / Europe

Some services we use (for example Google for forms, and CDN providers) may process a small amount of data outside the UK or EEA. Where the law requires it, they use approved safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses) under UK GDPR.

15. Your rights

  • Ask what limited data we hold about you (for example your email and form answers, if you gave them)
  • Ask us to correct or delete your contact details and form answers
  • Withdraw your consent at any time
  • Object to or limit certain processing where the law allows
  • Receive a copy of data you gave us directly, in a usable format
  • Complain to a data protection authority
