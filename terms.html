3. Who can use JetWages

The app is intended for cabin crew tracking their own pay and roster. You must be old enough to hold that role (at least 18) and only use your own staff number. Do not create an account for someone else or share your password.

4. Pay figures are estimates

JetWages helps you understand basic pay, sector pay, commission, extras, and deductions. Figures come from files you upload, calendar events you sync, and values you enter. They are estimates for your own records, not an official payslip, tax statement, or a promise of what your airline will pay.

Roster changes, swaps, delays, and airline rules can all move a month’s pay. You remain responsible for checking your employer’s documents. Do not rely on JetWages as the last word in a dispute with your airline, union, or tax authority.

5. Using the app without an account

Roster, pay history, sales, extras, Pie View, Stats, backups, and calendar sync run on your device. Optional encrypted cloud backup stores ciphertext we cannot read. Optional PDF parsing may send the file to our parsing server, which deletes it after reading — see the Privacy & Data Policy.

One account covers Swap, Friends, and optional cloud backup. Your staff number is the login identity. Work email is used to prove you are staff, and to send a code if you forget the sign-in password. It is not stored. You must keep your password and backup passphrase safe and tell us if you think the account has been misused.

We may suspend or close an account that is inaccurate, shared, abusive, or used in a way that breaks these terms or the law. You can ask us to delete the account by emailing data@jetwages.com. Uninstalling the app does not delete the account on its own.

The Swap board is a crew-to-crew matching tool. It is not your airline’s official swap process, crew portal, or a guarantee that a trade will be approved.

  • You may only post or offer duties that are yours, for the airline, base, and position you registered.
  • After an offer is accepted, the other staff number is shown so you can complete the swap with the airline, outside the app. Both of you should follow your company’s swap rules, rest, and reporting requirements.
  • JetWages is not a party to any swap, does not contact the airline for you, and is not responsible if a trade is refused, mistimed, or causes a roster or pay issue.
  • Do not use Swap to harass anyone, scrape the board, or post duties that are not yours.

8. Friends

Friends lets you pair with other crew so you can see matching days off and similar flying or ADTY start times. Only redacted clocks leave your device, encrypted so the Friends server cannot read them. Routes, pay, and sales are not shared.

Only pair with people you trust. You can give them a nickname on your phone; that nickname is not sent to us. Uninstalling the app creates a new identity key, so you will need to pair again unless you restored a backup that included the key.

9. Cloud backup
